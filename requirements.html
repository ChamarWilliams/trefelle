AI MENTOR · FOR NOW

Choose the AI that
powers your mentor.

Trefelle is unfunded, so there's no hosted AI mentor running on our bill yet. Until a subscription version exists, the mentor runs on an engine you choose and control—at no cost to us, and in most cases no cost to you either.

THREE WAYS TO RUN IT

Pick based on your
hardware, not ours.

01

Bring your own key

Connect an API key from a provider like OpenAI or Anthropic. Requests go straight from your browser to them—we never see the key or the bill.

02

WebLLM, in your browser

A small model downloads once and runs locally via WebGPU. No account, no key, no server—just your browser and your device.

03

A local model you already run

If you already have Ollama or LM Studio open, Trefelle can talk to it directly for the fastest, most private option.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS

What each option actually needs from your machine.

OPTION 01

Bring your own key

Best answer quality. Costs you per use, billed by the provider directly.

  • An API key from OpenAI, Anthropic, or a similar provider
  • Any modern browser, any device
  • An internet connection
  • A card on file with the provider for usage billing
Setup: ~2 minutes
OPTION 02

WebLLM in-browser

Free and private, with a lighter model and a one-time download.

  • Chrome or Edge 113+ (WebGPU required—no iOS Safari)
  • 8 GB+ RAM recommended
  • 2–4 GB free storage for the model download
  • A dedicated or capable integrated GPU helps a lot
Setup: one-time download
OPTION 03

Your own local model

Free, fast, and fully private—if you're already set up for it.

  • Ollama or LM Studio installed and running
  • A pulled model (e.g. Llama 3, Qwen 2.5, Mistral)
  • 16 GB+ RAM for comfortable 7B–8B models
  • Browser permission to reach localhost
Setup: for existing local-AI users

WHAT CHANGES LATER

A hosted, subscription mentor
is the long-term plan.

Once Trefelle can sustainably cover inference costs, a hosted mentor will remove the setup entirely—no key, no download, no local app. Until then, these three options keep the mentor free for us to offer and honest about what it costs you.
